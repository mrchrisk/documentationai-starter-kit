## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info">
Before starting, ensure you have:
- Windows 10 or later
- An internet connection for downloads and early access
- A BosOn account (sign up during early access request)
</Callout>

BosOn organizes your AI-generated images, videos, and assets. Follow these steps to get up and running in minutes.

## Get Started in 5 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request Early Access" icon="external-link">
    Visit the [BosOn early access page](https://getboson.app/#access) and submit your request.
    
    Early access opens soon for Windows users. You'll receive an email with your invite link and download instructions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download and Install" icon="download">
    Once approved, download the Windows installer from your invite email or [BosOn downloads](https://getboson.app/download/windows).
    
````bash
# Run the installer (double-click boson-setup.exe)
# Follow the on-screen prompts
# No admin rights required for standard install
````

    The app installs to `C:\Program Files\BosOn` by default.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch BosOn" icon="play-circle">
    Open BosOn from your Start menu or desktop shortcut.
    
    Sign in with your BosOn account. On first launch, grant folder permissions for imports.
    
    <Image
      src="https://getboson.app/media/boson-64.png"
      alt="BosOn app launch screen"
      width="400"
      height="300"
    />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Import Your First Files" icon="upload">
    Click **Library** in the sidebar, then **Import**.
    
    Select files from your computer, Google Drive, or ChatGPT exports.
    
    Supported formats: PNG, JPG, MP4, GIF (AI-generated assets work best).
    
    <Callout kind="tip">
      Organize imports by dragging to **Boards** like "Aura Launch" or "Spring Drop".
    </Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Your Initial Board" icon="settings">
    In the Library, click **New Board**.
    
    Name it (e.g., "My AI Assets"), add a description, and drag in your imported items.
    
    View stats: New, All, Status (Pending, Approved, Live).
  </Step>
</Steps>
