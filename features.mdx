## Overview

BosOn provides a comprehensive library for your AI-generated content. You make pictures and videos with familiar tools, keep everything organized in one place, find items quickly through visual search, and post them when ready. Core features include the Library for item management, status tracking across workflows, Boards for campaign organization, visual content search, and integration with Studio, models, and elements.

## Library and Item Management

The Library serves as your single source for all AI-generated items. You store images, videos, and elements here, with filters for new additions, all items, or recently added content. Organize thousands of assets without losing track.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Tag items during upload to enable quick filtering by type or source, such as `Gemini` or `ChatGPT`.
</Callout>

Use these steps to manage your library effectively:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload Content" icon="upload">
    Drag and drop files or integrate directly from AI tools.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Categorize Items" icon="tag">
    Assign labels like `Elements` or `BoardsResults`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export Downloads" icon="download">
    Generate links for Google Drive or other services.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Content Search and Discovery

Search your library by visuals, not just keywords. Upload an image to find similar content instantly, powered by AI matching. Filter by status, Board, or performance metrics like conversions `{>20}` or revenue `{>$100}`.

<Expandable title="Advanced Search Tips" default-open="true">
  Combine filters: `Live` status + `Aura launch` Board + visual similarity to a reference image. Export results as ZIP for quick sharing.
</Expandable>
