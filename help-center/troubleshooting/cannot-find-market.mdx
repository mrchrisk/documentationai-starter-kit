---
title: Can't Find My Market
description: How to fix missing markets in Market Trak.
---

## Symptoms

- Your market does not appear in the list
- Search returns no results

## Possible Causes

- Market was not created
- Filter hiding markets

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clear filters">
    Remove all filters in Markets view and search again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check creation">
    Confirm the market is listed under **"Recently Created"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh dashboard">
    Reload the Market Trak dashboard to sync data.
  </Step>
</Steps>
