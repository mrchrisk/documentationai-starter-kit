## Overview

Adding a new market helps you track performance and updates in BosOn Market Trak.

## Steps

1. Log in to your BosOn dashboard
2. Click **"Markets"** > **"Add Market"**
3. Fill in the market name and details
4. Click **"Save"**

Your market will now appear on your dashboard and can be managed or posted to at any time.
