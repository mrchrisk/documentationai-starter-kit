## Post an Update

Share a quick update to keep your market followers informed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select your market">
    Go to **Markets** and pick the market you wish to update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Post Update'">
    Find the **"Post Update"** button at the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and publish">
    Enter your message and hit **"Publish"**. Your update will appear instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>
